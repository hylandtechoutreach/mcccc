# Timing
The hope is that students complete the game between 7:15pm and 7:30pm. They should hit some of these milestones / checkpoints along the way. If they are running far behind these checkpoints, it may be necessary to provide extra guidance. These estimates could be wildly off, but the important thing is that the students can reach the end before 7:30 one way or another.
