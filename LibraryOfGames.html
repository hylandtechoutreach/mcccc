Library of Games

Library of Games

On this page, you'll find all sorts of games you can play. You can earn raffle tickets for:

  • Winning a Group Game
  • Getting a High Score (max 1)
  • Completing a Challenge (max 2)
  • Rating and Reviewing a Game (max 3)

Group Games

These games will be played live, and the winner (or winners) will earn raffle tickets!

  • Friendivia
  • Gimkit (random set)
  • Drawasaurus

High Score Challenges

For these games, the highest scorer will earn 3 raffle tickets (if there are 3 or more scores total). When you have a new high score, a facilitator must verify it. Your highest score will only count for a maximum of two games! Check the whiteboard for verified high scores. If there is none, that means any score at all will win you a ticket!

  • Finn's Ascent
  • Egg Hunt
  • Perfect Circle
  • MCC Platformer
  • Slope Game

Challenges

For these game challenges, anyone who completes them will earn a raffle ticket (maximum of 2).

Bottled: Catch a Rare Fish

Go to Bottled, go to any island, and start fishing! You will win a raffle ticket if you catch a Rare fish.

World's Hardest Game: First 3 Levels

Play the world's hardest game, and get through the first three levels!

Sokoban: Premsyl #57

Play through the basic sokoban lessons to get acquainted with the mechanics. There are several puzzles to play, but if you want a ticket, you'll have to beat Premsyl #57!

Password Game: Fifteen Rules

Create a password that matches the first 15 rules in the Password Game.

I'm Not a Robot: Fifteen Levels

Prove you're not a robot for fifteen levels in the I'm Not a Robot Game

Nonogram: Daily Challenge

Complete this daily nonogram puzzle.

Merge Blocks: Reach 2048

Play Merge Blocks, and get up to a 2048 block.

Red Remover: Beat 20 Levels

Play Red Remover and beat 20 levels.

Just For Fun: YouTube Playables

YouTube Playables are these weird browser-based games built into YouTube for some reason. Most of them seem like the kind of thing that would be featured in mobile game ads. Some of them are not very good, but some of them are surprisingly fun, and there are no ads! Here is a small selection to play.

  • Cubes 2048.io
  • Paper.io 2
  • Number Game
  • Flames & Fortune
  • Flow Blast
