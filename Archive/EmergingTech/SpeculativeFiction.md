# Speculative Fiction
Imagine a future in which some emerging or hypothetical technology has advanced beyond its current state. What will this technology be able to do? How might one individual be impacted by these new things? What is within the realm of possibility?

## Your Goal
Write a short story, journal entry, screen play, or anything else set in a near- or far-future world. When you're done, [click here to share your story.](https://forms.office.com/r/fYGeHrJrBP)

## Story Bones
Before starting, you will want to make some key decisions:

- What year is it?
- Where does the story take place?
- Who is the main character?
- Which technology is impacting the world?

If you want help, [click here for a random prompt generator](https://hytop.onrender.com/pf/specficprompts/).
