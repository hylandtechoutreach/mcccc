# Art Gallery
Create an online art gallery to show off all your cool work! [Click here to open the starter project.](https://hytop.onrender.com/e/my-art-gallery) Make a copy of it, and then update the `src` attributes of the `<img />` elements like so:

```html
<img src="[URL FOR YOUR IMAGE]">
```

You can also update the `href` attributes and the text of the `<a>` elements like so:

```html
<a href="[LINK TO YOUR WORK]">[TEXT FOR LINK]</a>
```

Feel free to add as many pictures and links as you'd like. You can change the content of the website in the **index.html** file, and change the website's appearance in the **style.css** file.
