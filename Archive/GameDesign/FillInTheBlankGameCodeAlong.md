# Fill-In-The-Blank Game Code-Along
In this activity, modify an existing Three.js game by changing some variables and adding a POWER-UP.

## Part Zero: Getting Started
[Click here to open the blank game.](https://hytop.onrender.com/e/three-js-blank-game)

Click the "Fork" button in the upper left to create your own copy of the project that you will be free to customize. Once you have that, **your first goal is to figure out how to win the game, and see if you can get a high score!** Don't worry about any of the files or code yet 🙂

You can click the "Open in new tab" button in the upper right (just above the gameplay preview) to play the game on a full screen.
