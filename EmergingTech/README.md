# Emerging Technology & Society
This lesson introduces some concepts around emerging technology, and explores their impact on humanity.

### Agenda

| Activity | Time |
|-|-|
| Warm-Up | 10m |
| Presentation | 15m |
| Wiki Races | 10m |
| Break | 10m |
| Copilot Learning | 10m |
| Speculative Fiction | 20m |
| Gimkit | 15m |
| Self-Paced Exploration | 30m |

## Warm-Up: Infinite Craft
Students should play [infinite craft](https://neal.fun/infinite-craft/) for a while. This is a game that uses A.I. (which is an emerging technology). It is one example of how A.I. has changed things - rather than being bound by human imagination, this game can go on forever, coming up with unique and novel content endlessly.

## Presentation
Present the [Emerging Tech PowerPoint](EmergingTech.pptx). These slides introduce the concept of emerging technology, briefly cover a few examples, and lead into the next activities.

## Activity: Wikiracing
For this activity, students will start on one Wikipedia article, and try to make it to another wikipedia article while clicking only blue links. Whoever does it fastest wins!

Ask the group for suggestions of articles, and go from one to the other. Once someone is done, they will have to click the "back" button through their whole path.

## Activity: Copilot Learning
For this activity, students should use Microsoft Copilot to learn about any topic they would like. They should type questions, and make sure they understand the output. When they feel they have learned sufficiently, they can ask Copilot to quiz them!

## Activity: Speculative Fiction
For this activity, students are tasked with imagining possible futures that are impacted by emerging technologies. They can choose any year, setting, character, and technology. Then, they should write out some basic ideas of what could happen.

## Gimkit
First, allow students to practice the Gimkit quiz questions (from lesson homepage). Some of the items are things that were not covered. Then, start the [quiz game](https://www.gimkit.com/view/69c167196c17bfd58b244a9d).

## Self-Paced Time
With any remaining time, students can explore the gallery, resources, etc.
