# Speculative Fiction
Imagine a future in which A.I. has advanced beyond its current state. What will A.I. be able to do? How might one individual be impacted by these technologies? What is within the realm of possibility?

## Your Goal
Write a short story, journal entry, screen play, or anything else set in a near- or far-future world. You are welcome to write this in any medium you'd like, but feel free to use [this project](https://hytop.onrender.com/e/spec-fic-start) as a starter - it will provide a whole website you can edit that even reads the story out loud for you!

When you're done, share your story using the form linked on the [club homepage](../BOOKREADME.md).

## Story Bones
Before starting, you will want to make some key decisions:

- What year is it?
- Where does the story take place?
- Who is the main character?

If you want help, [click here for a random prompt generator](https://hytop.onrender.com/pf/aispecfic/).
