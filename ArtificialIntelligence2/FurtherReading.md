### The Turing Test
A landmark paper in the early study of artificial intelligence. Designed by Alan Turing, it was meant to test if a machine had achieved actual intelligence.

- [Read about the test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_test)
- [Take the test live](https://turingtest.live/play/)

### ELIZA
One of the earliest and most significant examples of a chatbot, ELIZA was developed from 1964 to 1967 at MIT by Joseph Weizenbaum.

- [The ELIZA effect: a tendency to project human traits — such as experience, semantic comprehension or empathy — onto rudimentary computer programs having a textual interface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ELIZA_effect)
- [Chat with ELIZA](https://web.njit.edu/~ronkowit/eliza.html)

### A.I. Defeats a Chess Grandmaster
[In 1997, Deep Blue became the first computer to defeat a world champion in Chess.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_Blue_versus_Garry_Kasparov)

## Technology
The technologies involved in artificial intelligence are interesting and varied. Generally, when people say "A.I." in 2025, they are referring to [generative A.I.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_artificial_intelligence). Almost always, these tools are built on [large language models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_language_model) or involve some form of [deep learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning).

- [Essential Terminology](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-vs-machine-learning-vs-deep-learning-vs-neural-networks)
- [A.I. Learns to play Super Mario World](https://youtu.be/ldXxDNjS5jw)
  - This is made possible by **Reinforcement Learning**: one subset of ML
- [A.I. Agents](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-agents)

### Computer Brains
A.I. systems often closely resemble humans in their ability to communicate. [Neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_network) serve as the basis for deep learning, which powers most A.I. tools today.

- [Cognitive Revolution: psychology inspired by computers, inspiring computers inspired by psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_revolution)
- [Complete map of a Fruit Fly brain](https://www.science.org/content/article/complete-map-fruit-fly-brain-circuitry-unveiled)
