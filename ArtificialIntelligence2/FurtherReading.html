The Turing Test

A landmark paper in the early study of artificial intelligence. Designed by Alan Turing, it was meant to test if a machine had achieved actual intelligence.

  • Read about the test
  • Take the test live

ELIZA

One of the earliest and most significant examples of a chatbot, ELIZA was developed from 1964 to 1967 at MIT by Joseph Weizenbaum.

  • The ELIZA effect: a tendency to project human traits — such as experience, semantic comprehension or empathy — onto rudimentary computer programs having a textual interface
  • Chat with ELIZA

A.I. Defeats a Chess Grandmaster

In 1997, Deep Blue became the first computer to defeat a world champion in Chess.

Technology

The technologies involved in artificial intelligence are interesting and varied. Generally, when people say "A.I." in 2025, they are referring to generative A.I.. Almost always, these tools are built on large language models or involve some form of deep learning.

Computer Brains

A.I. systems often closely resemble humans in their ability to communicate. Neural networks serve as the basis for deep learning, which powers most A.I. tools today.
