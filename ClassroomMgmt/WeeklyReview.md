# Community Agreements: Weekly Review
At the start of each session, the established community agreements will be reviewed. They will be:

- Posted on the wall with a big post-it
- Cycling through the opening slides before & during dinner
- Mentioned and shown on a PowerPoint slide before the evening's structured activity

There will be a clear visual cue indicating that it is time to focus on the lesson. That means no more games, no more extraneous side conversations, no more getting up without raising a hand. Monitors must remain off during direct instruction, and students must not stray during computer-based activities.

The plan is to have one hour of focus time each evening, from 5:30-6:30pm.
