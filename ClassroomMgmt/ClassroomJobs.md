# Classroom Jobs
It can be helpful to provide students with specific roles and responsibilities in the classroom. By doing these jobs, they can earn tickets to be redeemed (set at market rate).

## New Student Ambassador
This experienced student will be responsible for the experience of a newly joined student. They will be friendly, provide basic club information, and answer any questions of the new student. They must be hospitable and welcoming above all.

- Go over all the [pertinent information on the club homepage](../BOOKREADME.md)
- Help the new student make and display a name tent
- Show the new student where to get drinks and snacks and wash hands
- Sit next to the new student and support them throughout the session
- Get to know the new student by asking them questions, such as:
  - Where do you go to school?
  - How did you hear about this club?
  - Have you done any coding before?
  - Do you do anything else outside of school?
  - Do you like Roblox / Five Nights at Freddy's / Minecraft / Fortnite / Whatever?

## Teaching Assistant
If a student has already participated in a lesson, or if they already know the content, they can help out as a teaching assistant. This will primarily apply during any self-paced or guided activities. Teaching assistants may:

- Help deliver a lecture, or explain a topic
- Walk around the room during an activity, and...
  - ...ask if anyone needs help
  - ...ask students to talk about what they're doing
  - ...offer positive comments and encouragement
- Talk about a tangential topic
- Show off something cool that is related to a topic
- Help find relevant videos or games

## Shopkeeper
The shopkeeper will be responsible for the exchange of tickets for rewards. When the shop is open, students (customers) will come to the shopkeeper and ask for goods. The shopkeeper will check the number of tickets offered, take them, and provide the customer with the item. If customers attempt to haggle, it is up to the shopkeeper to decide what is reasonable and what is not. At the end of a shopkeeper's shift, the items will be checked against the back of tickets.

## Snack & Drink Delivery Service
This student will take orders from other students, and go back to the dinette to pick up their requested items. This should happen at semi-regular intervals (every ~15 minutes or so). Students who receive the items are encouraged (but not required) to tip.
