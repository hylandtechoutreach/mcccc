# New Student Ambassador
This experienced student will be responsible for the experience of a newly joined student. They will be friendly, provide basic club information, and answer any questions of the new student. They must be hospitable and welcoming above all.

- Sit next to the new student and support them throughout the session
- Go over all the [pertinent information on the club homepage](../BOOKREADME.md)
- Get to know the new student by asking them questions, such as:
  - Where do you go to school?
  - How did you hear about this club?
  - Have you done any coding before?
  - Do you do anything else outside of school?
  - Do you like Roblox / Five Nights at Freddy's / Minecraft / Fortnite / Whatever?
