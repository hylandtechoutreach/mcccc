# Tickets
Earn tickets by abiding by the [non-negotiables](./NonNegotiables.md) (and other ways too)!

- **1 ticket:** 1 piece of candy or 1 sticker
- **2 tickets:** 1 small toy (like a rubber duck)
- **3 tickets:** 1 Dr Pepper or bag of Doritos
- **10 tickets:** 1 big toy (like a plush)

## Guidelines
Tickets are only redeemable when the shop is open.

There will be a limited number of tickets in circulation at all times.

Tickets can be traded among students as desired.
