Classroom Environment

Non-Negotiable Community Agreements

These tenets apply to both students and facilitators.

Respect People

Students and facilitators

Respect Space

Classroom and equipment

Respect Time

Focus and fun

Include Everyone

No one left out, share experiences

Engage Meaningfully

Participate, collaborate, and pay attention

Learn & Grow

Explore new ideas, adjust after mistakes, and keep improving

Take Responsibility

Be accountable for actions and the consequences that follow, be in charge of yourself, set an example for others, lead

Spread Positivity

Be kind, friendly, uplifting and encouraging

Be Resilient

Do not be afraid to fail, ask questions, and try things even if it is hard
