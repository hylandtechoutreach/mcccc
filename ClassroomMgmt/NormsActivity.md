# Norms Activity
This activity is meant to help the members of the classroom strengthen their commitment to the community agreements, and empower them to augment and shape them.

## Questions
Each student should answer these questions:

- What do each of these tenets mean to you?
- What do you expect from your fellow club members?
- What do you expect from club facilitators?
- Are there any norms you would like to add to our classroom?
