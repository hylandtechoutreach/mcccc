# Snack & Drink Guidelines
Snacks and drinks are theoretically unlimited - but be reasonable. It's kind of like how I have "Unlimited PTO"

~2 drinks per night, ~4 snacks per night will allow _everyone_ to share, and make sure we don't go over budget.

Different items may be available upon request.

Please wash your hands after eating any snacks that make your fingers greasy or cheesy.
