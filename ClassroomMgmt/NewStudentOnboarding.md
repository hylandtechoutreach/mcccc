# New Student Onboarding
Any student who joins will go through a basic on-boarding process.

## Re-Introductions
To ensure inclusivity, it will be important for all members of the club (students as well as facilitators) to re-introduce themselves to each new student who joins. This can be as simple as everyone saying their name and basic facts, or it can be through a "get-to-know-you" type of icebreaker activity.

## Community Agreement Review
With each new student, it will be important to maintain the standards of the classroom environment. The community agreements should be reviewed thoroughly to set the tone with each newcomer, and keep everything in line.

## New Student Ambassador
Each new student will be assigned an ambassador to help them get to know our club. Theoretically any returning student can become an ambassador, but specific students might be better suited to the role, or might benefit the most from the responsibility. More on the [jobs page](ClassroomRoles.md).
