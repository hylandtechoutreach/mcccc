Escalation

Escalation Framework

This is always meant to be restorative, never punitive! The only goal is to create a positive classroom environment for all who attend our clubs.

Levels

Generally, each new incident will climb one level up. The hope is that the lower levels mitigate the need to escalate to the upper levels.

Level 1: Redirect

Brief reminder of the agreement that applies.

Level 2: Reflection

Conversation about what happened, which agreement was impacted, and how to make a better choice.

Level 3: Repair

Student takes action to repair the impact of their behavior.

Level 4: Parent/Guardian Communication

Used when concerns continue or become more significant.

Level 5: Temporary Removal

For repeated or serious behavior that disrupts safety or the learning environment, with a re-entry conversation before returning.

Level Skipping

Some actions will have immediate consequences, and will not be tolerated. These actions will jump straight to Levels 4 and 5:

  • Bullying
  • Harassment
  • Threats
  • Inappropriate / Unsafe Use of Tech
