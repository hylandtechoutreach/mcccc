## Display
If you make or find some ASCII art and you want to save it and share it, you can use HyTop!

1. Copy your ASCII art onto your clipboard (`Ctrl`+`c`)
1. [Open this project](https://hytop.onrender.com/e/ascii-art-display)
1. Copy the project to make your own version
1. On the left, open the **asciiart.txt** file
1. Paste your ASCII art into the file (`Ctrl`+`v`)

You should see your art display on the site! You may need to open the preview in a new window to see the whole thing.

**BONUS:** open the **script.js** file to change the background color and text color, or open the **index.html** file to change the page title and more.
